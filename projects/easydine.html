Easy Dine

An iOS & Google play store app for recipes, weekly meal plans and grocery lists that price themselves out.

01Overview

Easy Dine covers the whole loop from "what's for dinner?" to the shopping list. Browse a recipe library personalized to your goals, or search by whatever is already in your fridge. Every recipe comes with ingredients, equipment, step-by-step instructions, nutrition and an estimated cost for the dish.

Plan the week by dragging recipes onto days, see each day's calories and cost at a glance, then generate one combined grocery list with an estimated price for every item.

02Features

  • Personalized recipe feed

    A full library with suggestions built around what you're trying to eat.

  • Search by ingredient

    Type what you have on hand and find recipes that use it.

  • Full breakdowns

    Ingredients, equipment, steps, nutrition and an estimated cost per dish.

  • Weekly planner

    Drag recipes onto days with daily calorie and cost totals.

  • Priced grocery list

    One combined list from your plan, with an estimated price per item.

  • Community recipes

    Publish your own recipes under your name for anyone to find.

  • Reel to recipe (Pro)

    Paste an Instagram or YouTube link, or upload a clip, and the app drafts the title, ingredients and steps.

  • Recipe remix (Pro)

    Ask for lower-calorie, gluten-free or anything else, and it rewrites the recipe for you to review.
